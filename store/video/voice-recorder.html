LearnLoop voice-over recorder

Record each line of the guide video in your own voice. Your recordings stay in this browser until you download them. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Use Chrome or Edge, in a quiet room, with the phone or laptop about an arm's length away.
  • Press Record, wait a moment, read the line calmly, then press Stop. Redo any line as often as you like.
  • Try to stay close to the time shown. If a line runs longer, the video scene will be lengthened to fit.
  • When every line has a tick, press Download all recordings and send the zip file to Claude.
