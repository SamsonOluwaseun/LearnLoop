**Early test version of LearnLoop for Android. Not on Google Play yet.**

This app has been built automatically from the LearnLoop repository. Its screens have been tested in a browser at phone size, but the app has not yet been tried on a real phone. Please install it only to help test, and tell us what happens: https://github.com/SamsonOluwaseun/LearnLoop/issues

How to install:
1. Download LearnLoop-android-preview.apk below on your Android phone (Android 8 or later).
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser for this install.
3. Open LearnLoop, set a parent PIN and follow Phone setup.

To update, uninstall the old version first. Each test build has a different signing key. Uninstalling deletes the app's data.

What it does, what it cannot do and how it protects privacy: https://github.com/SamsonOluwaseun/LearnLoop/tree/main/mobile
