# Curriculum map

LearnLoop's questions follow what children meet at school in England. Each skill below is linked to a statement in the official curriculum. The questions are original. LearnLoop does not copy any scheme, app or textbook.

- [Early years foundation stage statutory framework for group and school-based providers](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-years-foundation-stage-framework--2), in force from 1 September 2026. Early learning goals for Number, Numerical Patterns, Word Reading and Comprehension.
- [The national curriculum in England: mathematics programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), key stages 1 and 2.
- [The national curriculum in England: English programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study), key stages 1 and 2, including spelling appendix 1 (common exception words, the years 3 and 4 word list, prefixes, suffixes and homophones) and grammar appendix 2.

## How the starting year is chosen

In England the school year runs from 1 September to 31 August, and a child starts Reception in the September after their fourth birthday. LearnLoop uses the date of birth to work out the school year, then starts each subject there. A child not yet at school starts at Reception. LearnLoop currently goes up to Year 3.

After the start, only results move things. A birthday or a new school year never makes the questions harder on its own.

## How questions get harder

Every skill has three levels. Level 1 is the gentlest version of the curriculum statement and level 3 is the fullest.

- **A skill moves up a level** when at least 5 of the child's last 6 answers on it were given without any help, across at least 2 different sessions.
- **A skill moves back a level** when 2 or fewer of the last 6 answers were given without help, across at least 2 sessions.
- **The year moves up** for a subject when three quarters of that year's skills have reached level 3.
- **The year moves back** when half of that year's skills are at level 1 and still a struggle across several sessions.
- Answers given after a clue, another way of looking at it, or a worked example never count towards moving up.
- Each session starts with skills to practise again, then skills not seen for a week, then the rest.
- When a question is too hard three times, the child moves on and the skill comes back later with an easier example.

Every change is written to the parent report with its reason, for example: *Taking away within 20 moved up to level 2: 5 of the last 6 answers were given without help, over 2 sessions.* Parents can set the level for each subject by hand in **Children and rules**.

These rules are starting points for a family pilot, not proven teaching rules.

## Not covered yet

- Writing, handwriting and composing sentences (LearnLoop only asks children to tap or type short answers).
- Formal phonics assessment. The first-sound and word-reading activities use pictures and are practice, not a phonics check. Windows voices cannot say pure letter sounds reliably, so LearnLoop speaks example words instead.
- Geometry, measures of length, mass and capacity, and statistics.
- Year 4 and above.
