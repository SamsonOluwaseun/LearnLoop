# Privacy

LearnLoop keeps everything in Chrome's storage on your laptop. It sends nothing over the internet.

**What it keeps:** children's display names, starting level, an optional birthday, rules, measured video seconds per day, learning results per skill and per session, parent overrides, and a hash of the parent PIN and recovery code.

**What it never keeps:** video titles, channel names, browsing history, screenshots, keystrokes, voice recordings or anything typed outside LearnLoop. To return a child to their video it saves only that video's address (just the video ID) and the position, and replaces them at the next switch. The CSV export does not include video addresses.

**Deleting:** the parent page has **Data and PIN > Delete** for one child or for everything. Removing the extension deletes all its data.

**Retention:** the last 500 learning sessions and 120 days of daily totals. Older rows are removed automatically.

As the parent, you decide whether to use LearnLoop with your children. If a hosted service or email reports are added later, they will need a separate privacy review first.
